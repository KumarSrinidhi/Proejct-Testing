Design a modern mobile home/landing screen for **Campus Runner** — an on-campus errand and delivery app for students.

**App context:**
Campus Runner connects students who need errands done (customers) with other students who fulfill those tasks (runners). Think fast food pickup, item delivery across campus, printing, etc.

**Page Structure:**
1. Top bar with app logo "Campus Runner" and a location/campus chip
2. Hero section: bold headline ("Get anything delivered on campus") with a vibrant CTA button ("Post a Task")
3. Category quick-access row: icons for Food, Packages, Printing, Groceries, Other
4. "Active Orders" section (show 1–2 card placeholders with status indicators)
5. "Nearby Runners" horizontal scroller with avatar + rating chips
6. Bottom navigation bar: Home, Orders, Messages, Profile

**Design direction:**
- Energetic, youth-friendly, campus vibe
- Dark mode preferred with vibrant accent color (electric blue or neon green)
- Rounded cards, subtle glassmorphism on stat cards
- Modern sans-serif typography (Inter or Outfit)
- Mobile screen (390px wide)
